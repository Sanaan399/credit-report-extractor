This report uses a 1-100 point international credit-risk score with letter
rating bands (A+/A/BB/B/C/NR) explained under "BASIS OF CREDIT RATING" or
"CREDIT RECOMMENDATION". That section is a generic lookup table (each row
gives a point range and its letter code) -- it does not print this specific
company's letter rating on its own. Find "POINTS ALLOCATED: NN" for this
company, match NN against that table's point ranges, and use the
corresponding letter as credit_rating and rating_band. This is reading a
printed table, not guessing -- always fill in credit_rating this way when
points_allocated and the table are both present. risk_level is the separate
plain-English/word label (e.g. "LOW", "Medium") shown near "Risk Level" --
do not put that word in credit_rating, and do not put the letter rating in
risk_level.

Registration details are under "REGISTRY DATA" or "Key Facts".
Management/shareholder info is under "MANAGEMENT" / "SHAREHOLDER
INFORMATION". Note: this PDF's underlying text can have labels and values
misaligned from a two-column layout -- read the page visually rather than
assuming raw left-to-right text order is correct.
