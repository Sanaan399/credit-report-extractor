This is a GLADTRUST "ORDER DETAILS & INVESTIGATION RESULTS" report, typically
on a Chinese company. It uses a letter CREDIT RATING (A-F) plus a numeric
score out of 100, and a separate CREDIT LIMIT / base credit limit figure.
Registration details are under "REGISTRATION INFORMATION" (unified social
credit code, registered capital, legal representative). The company's
Chinese name often fails to extract from the PDF's text layer -- use the
English name/translation given instead.
